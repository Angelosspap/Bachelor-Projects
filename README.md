# Bachelor Projects | Statistical Analysis

A collection of undergraduate academic projects completed during my BSc in Statistics and Insurance Science at the University of Piraeus, Greece.

These projects focus on statistical analysis, hypothesis testing, regression, and data visualization.

## 1. Factors Associated with Life Satisfaction (2021)

**Course:** Statistics of Social Phenomena  
**Tools:** IBM SPSS Statistics

This project examines factors associated with life satisfaction among 3,216 individuals aged 50 and over in Denmark, using data from 2017.

**Methods:**
- Descriptive statistics and data visualization
- Cross-tabulation and chi-square tests
- Binary logistic regression

The analysis explores relationships between life satisfaction and demographic, socioeconomic, and health-related factors. The findings highlight financial circumstances and self-reported health as important factors associated with life satisfaction.

## 2. The Impact of COVID-19 on Mortality and Life Expectancy (2021)

**Course:** Special Topics in Actuarial Science  
**Type:** Group project  
**Tools:** R (data visualization)

This project investigates the impact of COVID-19 on mortality and life expectancy across five European countries: Belgium, Denmark, Greece, Italy, and Finland.

**Methods:**
- Descriptive statistical analysis and visualization
- Normality testing and non-parametric statistical tests
- Correlation analysis
- Mortality indicators and life expectancy comparisons

The study compares COVID-19 cases, deaths, excess mortality, and life expectancy across countries and age groups.
