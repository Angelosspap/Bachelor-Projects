# Life Expectancy Visualizations

This directory contains three comparative visualizations reproduced from Chapter 5.1 of the original bachelor's thesis (University of Piraeus, 2021).

## Figures

- **`female_life_expectancy.png`** - Female life expectancy across Belgium, Denmark, Greece, Italy, and Finland (2005–2019).
- **`male_life_expectancy.png`** - Male life expectancy across the same five countries.
- **`total_life_expectancy.png`** - Overall life expectancy comparison across the five countries.

The figures correspond to Figures 5.1–5.3 of the original thesis and were generated using R, `ggplot2`, and `ggthemes`.

For the full methodology and interpretation of results, see the [main analysis README](../README.md).
